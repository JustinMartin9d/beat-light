Tips

  • Play the music from a speaker, TV or live band in the room. Music playing on this same iPhone may pause when the microphone turns on.
  • Keep this page open with the screen unlocked. iOS turns the light off when you lock the phone or switch apps.
  • Nothing is recorded or uploaded. The camera is only used to switch the flashlight.
  • Fast flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.
